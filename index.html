01Inventory Analytics

Agri-Supply Inventory Intelligence

Built an inventory intelligence system for an agri-supply company using a 5-table PostgreSQL database, 20+ SQL business queries, Python feature engineering, and a 3-page Power BI dashboard.

Key findingCapital locked in dead stock
₹3.4 Crlocked in dead stock while critical items ran out
Needed to restock critical items₹1
Frozen in dead stock₹34
2.9%of dead stock funds all urgent reorders
₹2.05 Crrecovered at a 40% discount

Problem
₹3.4 Crores locked in dead stock while fast-selling products were constantly running out.
Insight
For every ₹1 needed to restock critical items, ₹34 sat frozen in dead stock. Liquidating just 2.9% of dead stock would fully fund all urgent reorders.
Business Impact
Selling dead stock at a 40% discount recovers ₹2.05 Cr, enough to fund every reorder, and addresses ₹1.1 Cr in annual warehouse carrying costs.

03Supply Chain Analytics

GreenYield India: Operations Audit

Business simulation as Lead Data Analyst for a scaling startup. Conducted a 3-phase audit: baseline diagnostics, root cause SQL analysis, and prescriptive action plan.

Key findingDefect losses · ₹5.19L
81.92%of defect losses came from one supplier
  • Bharat Yield Corp 81.92%
110%Warehouse capacity in use
916Orders pushed into cross-regional shipping
Problem
Profitability cracked under scale: hidden supplier defects, 110% warehouse capacity, and 15% of orders bleeding into costly cross-regional fulfillment.
Insight
One supplier drove 81.92% of defect losses (₹5.19L). Bengaluru's capacity crisis forced 916 orders into cross-regional shipping, slowing delivery 3.5×.
Business Impact
Recommended supplier suspension and a 3PL leasing plan for overflow. Identified a potential recovery of ₹5.19 Lakhs in annual defect losses.
