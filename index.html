OPERATIONS AUDIT
Supply Chain Analytics
GreenYield India — Supply Chain & Operations Audit
A full business simulation where I acted as Lead Data Analyst for a scaling agribusiness startup. Conducted a 3-phase data audit — baseline diagnostics, root cause SQL analysis, and a prescriptive action plan — across supplier quality, warehouse capacity, and logistics performance.
Problem Revenue was growing but profitability was cracking under scale — hidden supplier defects, a warehouse operating at 110% capacity, and 15% of orders bleeding into costly cross-regional fulfillment.
Insight One supplier alone — Bharat Yield Corp — was responsible for 81.92% of all defect-related losses (₹5,19,057). Bengaluru's capacity crisis was forcing 916 orders into cross-regional shipping, making delivery 3.5× slower than normal.
Python PostgreSQL Power BI DAX CTEs Gemini
💼 Business Impact
Recommended immediate suspension of Bharat Yield Corp, a short-term 3PL leasing plan for Bengaluru overflow, and a long-term warehouse upgrade — eliminating the capacity crisis and recovering ₹5.19 Lakhs in annual defect losses.

AGRI-SUPPLY CHAIN
Inventory Analytics
Agri-Supply Inventory Intelligence — Dead Stock & Reorder Optimization
Built a complete inventory intelligence system for an agri-supply company — 5-table PostgreSQL database, 20+ SQL business queries, Python feature engineering, and a live-connected 3-page Power BI dashboard that tells management exactly what to stock, what to clear, and when to reorder.
Problem The company thought they had "too much inventory." The data revealed a deeper truth — they had the wrong inventory. ₹3.4 Crores locked in dead stock while fast-selling products were constantly running out.
Insight For every ₹1 needed to restock critical items, the company had ₹34 frozen in dead stock — a capital misallocation problem, not a cash problem. Liquidating just 2.9% of dead stock would fully fund all urgent reorders.
PostgreSQL Python SQLAlchemy Pandas Power BI DAX ClaudeAI
💼 Business Impact
Identified ₹3.4 Cr in dead stock with a clear recovery path — selling at 40% discount recovers ₹2.05 Cr instantly, funds all reorders, and stops ₹1.1 Cr annual warehouse carrying cost bleed.

PERSONAL FINANCE
Finance Analytics
Personal Finance Intelligence — UPI Spending Analyzer
Built a full analytics pipeline on 1,000+ synthetic UPI transactions. Cleaned and engineered the data in Python, dug into spending patterns with Pandas and Seaborn, then brought everything together in a 3-page interactive Power BI dashboard.
Problem Most people have no clear picture of where their money actually goes month to month — they overspend without realising it.
Insight Shopping alone consumed 22.34% of the total budget, and Sunday was consistently the highest-spend day at ₹3,65,623 — the data pointed to a clear weekend impulse spending problem.
Python Pandas Seaborn Matplotlib Power BI DAX PostgreSQL
💼 Business Impact
Identified that cutting Shopping spend by just 20% could save ₹80,717 a year — and the Saving Score of 4.5/10 gave a clear, honest picture of spending discipline.
